# The Robot Sandwich

### Assumptions
**The bread will be placed on the table, outside of the bag; the robot will start with nothing on its hands. The peanut butter is on the fridge, and the butter knife is also on the table.**

### Instructions
1. The bread and the butter knife are on the table
2. Both of the robot hands are empty 
3. The robot will open the fridge using its left hand 
4. The robot will take out the peanut butter using its right hand 
5. The robot will open the peanut butter with both hands and remove the lid with its right hand
6. When done opening the peanut butter, the robot will put it back on the table 
7. Now the robot's hands are empty 
8. The robot will pick up the bread that it is already out of the bag with its left hand 
9. The robot will insert the butter knife on its right hand into the peanut butter jar and take some out
10. The robot will put peanut butter on the sandwich using the butter knife on its right hand with peanut butter on it
11. Everything is done once the peanut butter is spread on the sandwich using the butter knife that it is on its right hand.

### Done When
**Once the peanut butter is spread on the sandwich, this is the final step.**
