# The Robot Sandwich

## Instructions
1. The bread is on the table 
2. both of the robot hands are empty 
3. the robot will open the fridge using his left hand 
4. the robot will take out the peanut butter using his right hand 
5. the robot will open the peanut butter with both hands and remove the lid with his right hand
6. when done opening the peanut butter the robot will put it on the table 
7. now the robot hands are empty 
8. the robot will pick up the bread with his left hand 
9. the robot will insert the butter knife on his right hand into the peanut butter jar and take some out
10. the robot will put peanut butter on the sandwich using the butter knife on his right hand with peanut butter on it
11. done when the peanut butter is spread on the sandwich using the butter knife that it is on his right hand.
